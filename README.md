### RimWorld 1.6 Modding
I also develop mods for **RimWorld 1.6**. Each mod is written in C# against the game's modding API, using Harmony to patch game code at runtime.
- [Quadrunner-mod-rw](https://github.com/jtj9817/Quadrunner-mod-rw): Deploy autonomous utility, rescue, and combat robodogs to haul, recover downed colonists, and defend your base with zero DLC required.
- [tidy-threads](https://github.com/jtj9817/tidy-threads): Turn vanilla dressers and wardrobes into functional apparel-only storage for 20 to 30 pieces using Adaptive Storage Framework.
- [PracticeMakesDeadly](https://github.com/jtj9817/PracticeMakesDeadly): Add three target-practice recreation buildings that let colonists unwind while steadily training their Shooting skill.
- **ARC (`arc-mod-rw`)**: Extend the late game past Starflight with 53 institutional research projects across 13 mastery curricula, an author-aware work continuity queue, and an integrated ARC workspace.
- **Colony Web (`colony-web`)**: Visualize your colony's entire social history in an interactive relationship graph linking colonists, prisoners, animals, traders, and raiders through opinions, conversations, combat, and care.
- **Colony Command (`ColonyCommand`)**: Cut through late-game clutter by searching, filtering, and sorting the vanilla colonist bar by name, status, skill, and mood.
- **Fireteams (`fireteams`)**: Organize colonists into reusable tactical squads with gear search, assigned stations, and single-input squad orders.
- **Fresh Threads (`fresh-threads`)**: Automate colony wardrobe upkeep by matching apparel policies to existing stock without double counting and queuing vanilla tailoring bills for anything missing.
- **Queen Bee (`queen-bee-rw-mod`)**: Defend your perimeter with a deployable robot hive that launches specialized Scout, Explo, Electri, and Venom drone bees for patrol, combat, siege, and non-lethal crowd control.
